Complaint & Grievance Process

Last updated: April 2026

NBCC ACEP Requirement

As an NBCC Approved Continuing Education Provider (ACEP #7760), GA Integrated Therapeutic Perspectives LLC maintains a formal complaint and grievance process. All complaints are handled promptly, fairly, and confidentially.

1. Scope

This process applies to complaints related to:

  • Course content accuracy or quality
  • CE credit calculation or certificate issuance
  • Instructor conduct or qualifications
  • Platform accessibility or technical barriers
  • Billing, refunds, or subscription issues
  • Discrimination or ADA accommodation denials
  • Any alleged violation of NBCC ACEP standards

2. How to Submit a Complaint

Email (Preferred)

Send to support@counselorready.com with subject line "Formal Complaint".

Phone

(678) 664-4003 — Monday–Friday, 9 AM–5 PM ET

Please include:

  • Your full name and account email
  • Date(s) of the issue
  • Course name or code (if applicable)
  • Description of the complaint
  • Supporting documentation or screenshots
  • Your desired resolution

4. Appeals

If unsatisfied with the resolution, appeal within 10 business days by emailing support@counselorready.com with subject "Complaint Appeal – [Case Number]". Appeals are reviewed by Kejuiana Johnson, MA, LPC, NCC, CPCS, BC-TMH. A final decision will be issued within 10 business days.

6. Confidentiality & Non-Retaliation

All complaints are treated confidentially. CounselorReady strictly prohibits retaliation against any person who submits a complaint in good faith. Complaints and resolutions are retained for a minimum of 3 years per NBCC ACEP requirements.
