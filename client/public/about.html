Our Story

From Clinical Practice to Platform

As a Licensed Professional Counselor (LPC) in Georgia, I know firsthand the frustration of juggling continuing education requirements, tracking multiple credentials, and preparing for audits—all while trying to focus on what matters most: helping clients.

Spreadsheets weren't cutting it. Generic CE platforms didn't understand our specific needs. So I built something better.

CounselorReady combines everything mental health professionals need: NBCC-compliant continuing education courses, intelligent credential tracking, automated reminders, and audit-ready documentation—all in one place.

K. Johnson, LPC

NBCC-Compliant Education

ACEP Provider #7760

As an Approved Continuing Education Provider (ACEP), GA Integrated Therapeutic Perspectives LLC provides NBCC-compliant education recognized by the National Board for Certified Counselors.

What This Means For You:

  • All CE courses meet NBCC standards for quality continuing education
  • CE credits from our courses are accepted for NCC certification renewal
  • Most state licensing boards accept NBCC-compliant CE for license renewal
  • Courses not qualifying for NBCC credit are clearly identified on the platform
