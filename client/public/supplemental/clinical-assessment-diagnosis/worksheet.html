Part 1 — Biopsychosocial Assessment Checklist

Check each domain as addressed during the intake interview. Use the notes line to capture key findings, gaps, or areas requiring follow-up.

Presenting Concerns & History of Present Illness

Chief Complaint
Client's own words describing the primary reason for seeking services
History of Present Illness (HPI)
Onset, duration, precipitants, course, severity, functional impact, and prior episodes

Psychiatric & Medical History

Psychiatric History
Prior diagnoses, hospitalizations, outpatient treatment, and response to past interventions
Medical History
Current medical diagnoses, surgeries, neurological history, chronic conditions
Medications & Supplements
All current prescriptions, OTC medications, herbal supplements; doses; prescribing providers
Substance Use History
All substances (including alcohol, cannabis, nicotine); age of first use; frequency/quantity; periods of abstinence; consequences; treatment history

Developmental & Family History

Trauma History
Type(s) of adverse experiences, age of onset, duration, disclosure history, current trauma-related symptoms
Developmental History
Prenatal/perinatal events, developmental milestones, early attachment, academic history, special education needs
Family Psychiatric & Medical History
First- and second-degree relatives with psychiatric diagnoses, substance use disorders, suicide, or significant medical conditions

Social, Occupational & Cultural History

Social & Relationship History
Current living situation, significant relationships, social support network, relationship history, current sources of conflict or isolation
Occupational & Financial History
Employment status, history, job functioning, financial stressors, educational attainment
Legal History
Arrests, charges, convictions, probation/parole, restraining orders, pending legal matters
Cultural Considerations
Cultural identity, language, acculturation stressors, explanatory model of illness, religious/spiritual beliefs, cultural barriers to care
Strengths & Resources
Personal coping skills, community supports, spirituality, resilience factors, protective factors for safety planning
