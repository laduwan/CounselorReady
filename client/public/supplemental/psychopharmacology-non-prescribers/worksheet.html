Medication Monitoring & Prescriber Communication Worksheet

Psychopharmacology for Non-Prescribers  |  3 CE Hours  |  NBCC ACEP #7760
Purpose: This worksheet supports counselors in systematically tracking client medication experiences and preparing for coordinated care with prescribers. It is a clinical tool — not a diagnostic instrument. Always obtain signed ROI before contacting any prescriber. Do not advise clients to change or discontinue medications.
