Section 1 — Parts Inventory

Identify the parts active for this client. For each part, note the name/label the client uses, its apparent role or job in the system, where it shows up in the body, how the client feels toward this part (blended vs. Self-energy), and what exile/wound it may be protecting.

Part Name / Label Role or Job in System Body Location / Sensation Client's Feeling Toward This Part What It May Protect / Exile
Clinician prompt: "How do you feel toward that part right now?" — Responses like "I hate it," "I'm afraid of it," or "I want it gone" indicate blending. Responses like "I'm curious," "I feel for it," or "I want to understand it" indicate Self-energy present. Note which state is present for each part above.

Section 2 — Self-Energy Check-In (The 8 Cs)

Use these prompts to assess the client's current access to Self-energy. Ask each question aloud and note the client's response or your observation. Absence of a quality may indicate a part is blended.

"How calm do you feel inside right now, on a scale of 1–10?"
Calmness — Client Response / Observation
"Are you able to feel curious about this part, or does it feel threatening?"
Curiosity — Client Response / Observation
"How clearly can you see this part — or are other parts' reactions making it hard to see?"
Clarity — Client Response / Observation
"Do you feel any compassion for this part, even if it's small?"
Compassion — Client Response / Observation
"Do you feel like you can handle being with this part right now?"
Confidence — Client Response / Observation
"Are you open to new ways of relating to this part, or does it feel fixed?"
Creativity — Client Response / Observation
"Are you willing to go toward what this part holds, even if it's hard?"
Courage — Client Response / Observation
"Do you feel connected to yourself — or like parts are pulling you away?"
Connectedness — Client Response / Observation
Clinician note: If the client cannot access at least 3–4 Cs, consider returning to protector work or grounding before proceeding to exile access. Self-energy is a prerequisite, not an assumption.
