Section 2 — The Miracle Question

Scripted Prompt — read or paraphrase slowly:
"I want to ask you a question that may seem a little unusual. Suppose that tonight, while you are sleeping, a miracle happens. The problem that brought you here — it's gone. But because you were asleep, you don't know the miracle happened. When you wake up tomorrow morning, what would be the very first small thing you notice that tells you something is different? What would you see, feel, or hear that lets you know things have changed?"

Follow-up prompts: "Who else would notice? What would they see you doing differently? What would be different in your day by mid-morning? By evening?"
