Add Your First Credential

Start by adding your primary license. We'll track your CE requirements, renewal dates, and send you reminders so you never miss a deadline.

Why we need this

Your license number is required for the attendance records we submit to boards for compliance.

Pre-licensed? Enter PRELICENSED in the license field. Certificates will reflect your status at completion and cannot be changed retroactively — update your license number as soon as you're licensed.

⚠ If you skip this step, CounselorReady cannot advocate on your behalf in the event of a board audit. We strongly recommend completing this before starting any courses.

What you'll need:

  • • License type (LPC, LCSW, LMFT, etc.)
  • • License number (or "PRELICENSED")
  • • Expiration date
  • • State of licensure
