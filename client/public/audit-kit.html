What Auditors Look For

Most state licensing boards require three things during a CE audit:

1. Proof of completion — your CE certificates showing course title, CE hours, completion date, and provider approval number (NBCC ACEP #7760).

2. Content area mapping — evidence that your CE hours cover required categories like Ethics, Supervision, or your state's specific mandates.

3. Provider verification — confirmation that your CE provider is approved. CounselorReady operates under GAITP LLC, NBCC ACEP Provider #7760.

CounselorReady stores all of this automatically. Your audit package includes everything you need.
