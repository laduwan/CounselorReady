Building Therapeutic Rapport: The First Sessions

The therapeutic alliance is the most consistent predictor of positive outcomes across all therapeutic approaches. This practical 1-hour course focuses on the critical first sessions where rapport is established. Learn specific strategies for building trust, managing client anxiety, navigating the intake process while maintaining connection, and recovering when early sessions go poorly.

What you'll learn

  • Analyze the core components of therapeutic alliance and their impact on treatment outcomes
  • Demonstrate culturally responsive rapport-building techniques during initial client encounters
  • Evaluate signs of rapport breakdown and implement appropriate repair strategies
  • Apply evidence-based methods for establishing trust with diverse client populations
  • Synthesize research on therapeutic relationship factors that predict successful treatment engagement
  • Create individualized approaches for building rapport with resistant or ambivalent clients
