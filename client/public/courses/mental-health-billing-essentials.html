Mental Health Billing Essentials for Licensed Professional Counselors

This comprehensive 6-hour continuing education course provides licensed professional counselors with essential knowledge and skills in mental health billing practices. Participants will gain expertise in insurance credentialing, accurate coding systems, claims submission processes, and compliance requirements. The course covers practical strategies for managing denials, appeals, and patient billing while maintaining ethical standards and regulatory compliance. Through detailed instruction and real-world examples, counselors will develop competencies to optimize their practice's financial health and ensure proper reimbursement for services rendered.

What you'll learn

  • Analyze the fundamental components of mental health billing systems and their integration with clinical practice
  • Evaluate insurance verification processes and apply appropriate credentialing procedures for mental health providers
  • Demonstrate proficiency in selecting and applying CPT and ICD-10-CM codes for various mental health services and diagnoses
  • Apply claims processing procedures and troubleshoot common reimbursement issues in mental health billing
  • Evaluate billing practices for compliance with HIPAA, state regulations, and ethical standards in mental health care
  • Demonstrate effective patient financial management strategies while maintaining therapeutic relationships and professional boundaries
