The CBT Toolbox: Core Techniques for Clinical Practice

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy remains one of the most researched and effective approaches in mental health treatment. This comprehensive 3-hour course provides clinicians with a practical toolbox of CBT techniques for everyday practice. Through expert instruction and video demonstrations of real therapeutic techniques, participants will develop proficiency in core CBT interventions including cognitive restructuring, behavioral activation, exposure principles, and thought records. Each module includes video demonstrations to illustrate techniques in action.

What you'll learn

  • Analyze the core components of the cognitive model and its application to various mental health disorders
  • Evaluate common cognitive distortions in client presentations and differentiate between adaptive and maladaptive thought patterns
  • Demonstrate proficiency in implementing Socratic questioning techniques to guide client self-discovery
  • Apply cognitive restructuring interventions to challenge and modify dysfunctional thought patterns
  • Integrate exposure therapy principles within a cognitive-behavioral framework for anxiety-related disorders
  • Create structured CBT session frameworks that incorporate assessment, intervention, and homework assignments
