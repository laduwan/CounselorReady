Cultural Humility in Clinical Practice: Beyond Cultural Competence

This transformative 3-hour ethics course moves beyond checklist approaches to cultural competence toward cultural humility: a lifelong commitment to self-reflection, addressing power imbalances, and developing genuine partnerships with clients across cultural differences.

What you'll learn

  • Analyze the theoretical distinctions between cultural competence and cultural humility frameworks in clinical practice
  • Evaluate personal cultural assumptions, biases, and privileged identities that influence therapeutic relationships
  • Demonstrate culturally humble communication strategies that center client expertise about their own cultural experiences
  • Apply interventions for recognizing and addressing microaggressions in therapeutic settings
  • Create accountability structures for ongoing cultural humility development and self-reflection
  • Integrate culturally humble practices into treatment planning and case conceptualization processes
