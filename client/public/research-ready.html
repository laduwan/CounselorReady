Researched & Ready
Peer-reviewed research, transformed into continuing education
★
❧ ✦ ♦ ✦ ❧

Researched & Ready is a self-directed continuing education feature built for licensed counselors who want their CE grounded in current scholarship.

Search our library of open-access, peer-reviewed journal articles, select what's relevant to your practice, and submit them for CE credit. Once submitted, our system generates a structured clinical article and a 10-question posttest drawn directly from the research. Pass at 75% and your certificate and NBCC syllabus are issued automatically.

✦ What to expect: Articles are pulled from OpenAlex and filtered to psychology, counseling, and clinical psychology domains. CE hours are calculated at 1 hour per 6,000 instructional words. Most requests are approved and ready within 15 minutes.
